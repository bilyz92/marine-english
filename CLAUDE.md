## Product

The product sells short English courses to Vietnamese marine engine-room crew (thợ máy, sĩ quan máy), who mostly have weak English. It has two parts that share one account system and one database:

- a website for selling courses: catalogue, 3 free trial lessons per course, VietQR checkout, admin pages;
- a learning app built as a PWA (Android and iPhone) that must work offline on ships, syncing progress when Starlink is available.

All documents are written in Vietnamese and address the owner as "anh". The owner is a tanker chief engineer who is often at sea, and a helper on shore handles operations.

## Planned architecture (decisions made so far)

- **Hosting (decided 30/10/2026): one VPS, no Vercel, no Neon, no Cloudflare at the start.** The VPS runs the Next.js app (Docker, as in the owner's other projects), PostgreSQL and the media files. HTTPS via Let's Encrypt (Caddy or nginx); DNS at the domain registrar. The owner is responsible for backing up the database and the media folder together and for testing a restore before launch.
- **One Next.js codebase** serves the website, the PWA and the server API routes. Confirmed by the owner on 30/10/2026; the Express + SQLite code in Marine Academy is a separate project and is not the base for this one.
- **Media (decided 30/10/2026):** audio and images live on the VPS disk and are served under the site's own domain. All file access goes through one storage module so it can later move to Cloudflare R2 or another S3-compatible store, and Cloudflare can later be put in front as CDN without changing URLs. Downloaded lessons and audio are what learners study offline. Only marketing assets and the 3 free trial lessons are public; paid lesson files are downloaded only through entitlement-checked requests, never through stable public URLs.
- **Data:** PostgreSQL on the same VPS. Supabase was explicitly rejected, so do not reintroduce it. **Auth (decided 30/10/2026):** own database-backed sessions (decision D1, approved by the owner 30/10/2026; **Auth.js is not used**) with `oauth4webapi` for Google (the engine under `openid-client`, chosen in P1 because `openid-client` v6 rejects Google's alternate issuer `accounts.google.com`), email and Google sign-in, plus phone-number login with a one-time code sent by Zalo message (ZNS), because ships usually have Starlink internet but no mobile signal, so SMS codes often never arrive. SMS OTP is not used. Zalo OTP needs a verified Zalo Official Account and an approved message template, costs per message, and needs its own sign-in module; rate-limit code requests per phone number and per IP. Zalo codes reach only numbers that use Zalo; others sign in by email or Google, and the login screen must say so. Store phone numbers in one normalized form (+84…), unique in the database, so "0912…" and "+84912…" are the same person; convert to the ZNS provider's format only when sending. Learner email sign-in is proposed as a 6-digit code (matching the Login screen), separate from the admin email link. OTPs follow the lifecycle in `docs/design-gates-2026-10-30.md` section 3 (CSPRNG, stored only as an HMAC with a server-side pepper, purpose-bound, single use, wrong-attempt limit, layered rate limits, daily ZNS budget).
- **Two separate account models (decided 30/10/2026):** learner accounts and admin accounts never share a table, a session or a sign-in flow.
  - Learners live in `users` (the name used in the plan). Everything below about Zalo codes, account linking and the 2-device limit applies to learners only.
  - Admins (the owner and the helper on shore) live in `admin_users`, with a role such as owner or support. There is no self sign-up: the owner adds an admin's email to an allowlist. Admins sign in with Google or an email link, and only if their email is on the list; Google 2-step verification is recommended. The owner account cannot be deleted or demoted from the UI. Admin sessions use their own cookie and route (`/quan-tri`), separate from learner sessions.
  - `admin_audit_log` records the acting `admin_users.id`. A learner session can never call an admin API, and an admin session gives no learning access.
  - An admin who wants to study creates a normal learner account and receives access through a **non-payment grant** (reason required, never touches an order), not through payment reconciliation.
- **Account linking (decided 30/10/2026):** one account can hold several sign-in methods (phone via Zalo, email, Google). The learner links a new method from "Tài khoản của tôi" while signed in, after verifying that method. Never merge accounts automatically by matching names. If a learner already has two accounts, an admin merges them by hand: sign-in identities move to the target account and target progress rows are created from the best progress per lesson, but historical records (orders, enrollments, refunds, consents, learning events) stay attached to their original account; access and history are read through the merge group (target plus accounts merged into it). Decided 30/10/2026 after the design review; the merge is written to `admin_audit_log`.
- **Payments (decided 29/10/2026):** the checkout page shows the owner's existing static bank QR image, the amount and the order code as transfer content. SePay watches that bank account and sends a signed webhook; the system confirms automatically when the order code in the transfer content and the amount match. Wrong content, wrong amount or late money goes to "cần xử lý tay", and an admin matches it and grants manually. payOS is not used for now. Money goes straight into the owner's bank account. Isolate the gateway calls behind one module so the provider can be swapped (NFR-18).
- **Notifications:** email (Resend or Brevo) and optionally Zalo ZNS to learners; a Telegram bot to the business for paid orders, orders needing manual handling, a 21:00 daily summary, and a separate group for errors.
- **Scheduled jobs (proposed: a worker container in the same Docker Compose runs the scheduler, with no job endpoints exposed to the internet; each run is recorded in the database; set the time zone explicitly: with `Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh` the 21:00 summary is `0 21 * * *`, with UTC it is `0 14 * * *`; each job takes an atomic lock with an expiry so runs never overlap):** expire orders after 15 minutes, retry failed notifications, reconcile against SePay every hour, delete voice recordings past their retention period.
- **Speech and AI:** Azure Speech for speech and pronunciation assessment; Claude API is the existing proposed direction for conversation. Design a shared 3D assistant for website and PWA from the start, with approved lesson plans and Philippine/Indian/US English voices plus Vietnamese explanations. Release in stages after a one-character, one-lesson prototype; full conversation is a later stage, not implemented. Cloud conversation and pronunciation scoring require connectivity; downloaded lesson scripts and audio support offline study. Access must be checked server-side against enrollments and scoped tools, with admin-configurable permissions and usage limits; see section 7A of the latest working plan.

## Payment flow invariants

These rules are easy to break and matter most:

- The price always comes from the `courses` table on the server, minus at most one coupon. Never trust an amount sent by the client.
- An order only becomes "đã thanh toán" (paid) from a verified incoming transaction in `bank_transactions` that arrived through a signed SePay webhook, authenticated SePay API reconciliation, or an audited manual statement entry verified by the owner and matched by an admin. Non-payment grants never change an order. The browser's "success" page never unlocks a course. For SePay this means the HMAC-SHA256 authentication mode (`X-SePay-Signature` over the timestamp and raw body), not API Key or no auth, plus a check on `X-SePay-Timestamp` to reject replays.
- Order expiry is judged by the authoritative bank transaction time, never by when the transaction is received or verified, and the same rule and classifier apply to every verified source (webhook, API reconciliation, manual statement entry). SePay retries a failed webhook up to 7 times over about 33 minutes, so money sent within 15 minutes still pays the order even if it is reported late (decided 29/10/2026). The expiry job may already have marked such an order "hết hạn"; a verified transaction whose bank time falls inside the order's 15-minute window, with matching amount and code, moves it from "hết hạn" to "đã thanh toán". Only money whose bank time is after the window goes to "cần xử lý tay".
- Each bank transaction is processed once. `bank_transactions` is the only ingestion and deduplication point (unique on provider/bank, receiving account and transaction reference); the plan's `payments` table becomes `payment_allocations` (ledger row ↔ order, each ledger row allocated at most once), and outgoing refunds live in `refunds`. A replayed webhook must create no duplicate enrollment and no duplicate Telegram message. Every incoming transfer is first written to an immutable `bank_transactions` ledger; transfers that cannot be matched automatically (no code, wrong amount, late, duplicate) wait in a manual queue as ledger rows, not only as order states. **Paid-transfer reconciliation** by an admin must reference an unused bank transaction ID, and the system rejects an ID already used (decided 29/10/2026). A **non-payment grant** (internal study, gift, support) is a separate audited operation with a reason and never marks an order paid.
- Learning access is read only from `enrollments`, never inferred from `orders`. Each enrollment records its source (an order, or a non-payment grant). A refund must revoke the enrollment created by that order in the same transaction; learning progress is kept so a repurchase resumes it.
- Only a single transfer with exactly the order amount, exactly one valid order code, and a bank time inside the order window confirms automatically; everything else goes to the manual queue. Orders store an immutable server-side snapshot (course, price, coupon, final amount, `expires_at`). Payment ledger write, order lock and transition, enrollment and outbox events happen in one short database transaction; notifications go through a transactional outbox delivered after commit. Full rules: `docs/design-gates-2026-10-30.md` section 2.
- Notifications are sent after the course is unlocked, and a notification failure must never roll back the unlock.
- The order code is a random string that never repeats. Proposed format, pending owner confirmation: `ME` + 8 random uppercase letters/digits without look-alikes (0/O, 1/I/L), e.g. `ME7K3QX9AB`. Uniqueness comes from a unique constraint in the database plus regenerate on collision, not from randomness alone. The constraint covers every order, including soft-deleted ones, because the code appears in bank transfer content. Never use sequential numbers. Check SePay's payment-code recognition rules (prefix, length) when registering.
- Order states: chờ thanh toán (pending), đã thanh toán (paid), hết hạn (expired), cần xử lý tay (needs manual handling), đã hoàn tiền (refunded). Proposed after review (pending owner approval and the spec doc): keep only the four financial states and derive "cần xử lý tay" as a flag from unresolved queued transfers, so extra transfers never overwrite paid or refunded (`docs/design-gates-2026-10-30.md` §2.3). Wrong amount, wrong transfer content, or money arriving after expiry put the transfer into the "cần xử lý tay" queue; when the transfer identifies an order, that order is shown as needing manual handling until an admin reconciles or refunds it.
- Admin actions (manual grant, refund, price change) are written to `admin_audit_log`.
- An account can be logged in on at most 2 devices. What counts as a device, how a device is replaced, and the offline licence period are still open (gates G9 and G10 in `docs/design-gates-2026-10-30.md`).
